## 📝 Final Verdict

**Salt & Pepper, Belghoria** has rapidly established itself as one of the most popular dining destinations in the Belghoria–Dunlop area of North Kolkata. With a diverse multi-cuisine menu, cozy ambiance, friendly staff, and budget-friendly pricing, it caters to a wide audience — from college students looking for a hangout spot to families seeking a casual dining experience. While consistency in food quality across all items could be improved, the overall experience is highly regarded, as reflected in its stellar ratings across all major platforms.
